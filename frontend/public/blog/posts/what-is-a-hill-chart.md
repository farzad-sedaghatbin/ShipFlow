# What Is a Hill Chart?

A hill chart is a visual tool for tracking the real progress of a task — invented by Basecamp as part of the Shape Up methodology.

Unlike a burndown chart that shows tasks completed, a hill chart shows confidence. It answers the question every manager really wants to know: do we actually know how to solve this?

## The two phases of any task

Every piece of work has two phases:

**Uphill** — figuring out the approach. You are discovering unknowns, exploring solutions, making decisions. Progress here is uncertain.

**Downhill** — executing the known plan. You know what to build. You are just building it. Progress here is predictable.

A burndown chart treats both phases identically. A hill chart makes the distinction visible.

## Why this matters

The most dangerous moment in any project is when a task looks like it is 80% done but the team still has no idea how to approach the hard part. Hill charts expose this.

If a task has been sitting on the uphill side for two weeks, that is a signal — not a status update.
