# Betting Tables Explained: How to Prioritize Without a Backlog

The backlog is a graveyard of good intentions. Every team has one — hundreds of tickets nobody will ever touch, sorted by a priority system nobody trusts.

Shape Up replaces the backlog with a betting table. Every 6 weeks, stakeholders sit down and decide what gets built next. Pitches that don't get picked are not carried forward. They're gone.

## Why betting beats backlog grooming

Backlog grooming is maintenance work disguised as planning. You spend hours re-prioritizing tickets that were written months ago by people who may have already left the company.

Betting is different. You look at freshly shaped pitches — each one has a clear problem, a proposed solution, and a defined appetite. You decide: is this worth a 6-week bet?

If yes, a team picks it up. If no, the pitch expires. If it matters, someone will pitch it again next cycle with better framing.

## The discipline of saying no

The hardest part of betting is saying no. But that constraint is the point. When you can only pick 2-3 pitches per cycle, you are forced to focus on what actually matters.

Teams that adopt betting tables report fewer context switches, clearer priorities, and more work actually shipped.
