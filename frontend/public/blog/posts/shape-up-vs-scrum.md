# Shape Up vs Scrum: Which Is Right for Your Team?

Most software teams default to Scrum. Standups, sprints, backlogs, velocity charts. It works — until it does not.

Shape Up is the methodology Basecamp invented after years of frustration with Scrum. It throws out sprints, replaces backlogs with pitches, and gives teams 6-week cycles to solve problems their own way.

## The core difference

Scrum prescribes *how* you work: daily standups, sprint reviews, story points. Shape Up prescribes *what* you commit to: a shaped problem, a fixed time box, a small team.

In Scrum, the backlog grows forever. In Shape Up, unbuilt ideas expire. If something is important enough, it will be pitched again.

## When Scrum wins

- Large teams needing coordination across squads
- Regulated industries requiring predictable cadences
- Teams that want framework structure to reduce decision fatigue

## When Shape Up wins

- Product teams who want real ownership of outcomes
- Startups moving fast without ceremony overhead
- Teams tired of planning meetings that outnumber shipping days
