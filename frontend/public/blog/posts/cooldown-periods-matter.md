# Why Cooldown Periods Are the Secret to Sustainable Shipping

Six weeks of focused building is intense. Without a break, teams burn out. Without space to explore, innovation stalls.

Shape Up solves this with cooldown periods — typically 2 weeks between each 6-week cycle. During cooldown, there are no assigned projects. Teams choose what to work on.

## What happens during cooldown

Cooldown is not vacation. It is unstructured work time. Teams use it for:

**Bug fixes** — Small bugs that accumulated during the cycle get attention without disrupting shaped work.

**Technical exploration** — Engineers can prototype ideas, try new tools, or investigate technical debt without justifying it in a pitch.

**Learning** — Read documentation, take a course, pair with a teammate on something new.

**Shaping** — The best pitches often come from engineers who just finished building something. Cooldown gives them time to shape the next idea.

## Why most teams skip it (and regret it)

It feels wasteful to have engineers "idle" for two weeks. Managers want to start the next cycle immediately. But teams that skip cooldown consistently report higher turnover, more bugs in production, and declining morale.

The two-week investment pays for itself many times over.
