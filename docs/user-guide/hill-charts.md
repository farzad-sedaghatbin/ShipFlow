# Hill Charts

Hill charts provide a visual representation of work progress that goes beyond percentage complete.

## The hill metaphor

- **Left side (0–49%)** — figuring out: unknowns, discovery, design
- **Right side (50–100%)** — executing: building, shipping

A scope on the left side has unknowns. Once it crests the hill, the path is clear.

## Scopes

Scopes are discrete pieces of work within a pitch. Each scope becomes a dot on the hill chart.

Create scopes on a pitch's detail page. They automatically appear on the cycle's hill chart once the pitch is bet on.

## Updating positions

Drag dots left or right to update their position. Positions are saved immediately via the API.

## Reading a hill chart

- Many dots on the left = high discovery phase, more unknowns than expected
- Dots clustering near the top = team is in the figuring-out/execution transition
- Dots on the right = execution phase, clear path to done

## Pitch-level hill charts

Individual pitches also have their own hill chart showing scope progress for that specific pitch. Navigate to **Pitches → [pitch name] → Hill Chart**.
