# Reports & Analytics

## Available Reports
ShipFlow provides several built-in reports:

### Cycle Health Report
- Shows overall cycle progress
- Hill chart completion percentages
- At-risk pitches and stagnating scopes
- Team velocity metrics

### Team Velocity
- Tasks completed per cycle
- Average time to complete scopes
- Burndown-like view of scope completion

### Pitch Analytics
- Success rate of pitches (completed vs. cancelled)
- Average appetite vs. actual time spent
- Most common reasons for pitch rejection

## How to Access Reports
1. Navigate to **Reports** from the sidebar
2. Select the report type
3. Choose the time range and filters (cycle, team, etc.)
4. Reports can be exported or shared

# Wise Architecture (AI)

## What is Wise Architecture?
Wise Architecture is ShipFlow's AI-powered architecture advisor. It analyzes your pitches and suggests:
- Technology stack recommendations
- Architecture patterns (microservices, monolith, etc.)
- Risk assessment for technical decisions
- Integration suggestions

## How to Use Wise Architecture
1. Open a **Pitch** detail page
2. Click the **Wise Architecture** button
3. The AI analyzes your pitch's problem statement, solution, and any connected MCP context (codebase, designs)
4. Review the generated architecture recommendations
5. Accept, modify, or dismiss suggestions

## AI Risk Advisor
The AI Risk Advisor evaluates pitches for technical risk:
1. Open a pitch in the detail view
2. The risk score is displayed alongside the pitch details
3. Click to see risk breakdown: complexity, dependencies, unknowns
4. The advisor suggests mitigations for identified risks

# Project Types

## Supported Project Types in ShipFlow
- **Standard** — Full Shape Up process with all features
- **Small Batch** — Shorter cycles (2 weeks) for quick iterations
- **R&D** — Exploration-focused, lighter process

## Teams
Teams can be assigned to pitches and cycles:
1. Navigate to **Teams** from the sidebar
2. Create teams with designated members
3. Assign teams to pitches at the Betting Table
4. Track team workload across cycles

# Interactive Tour
ShipFlow includes a built-in interactive tour:
1. Click the **Help** icon (?) in the header
2. Select **Start Tour**
3. The tour highlights key features with step-by-step guidance
4. You can restart the tour at any time

# AI Assistant (Q&A)
The floating Q&A button provides context-aware AI assistance:
1. Click the **chat bubble** icon in the bottom-left corner
2. Ask any question about your current cycle, pitch, or project
3. The AI uses your organization's data to provide relevant answers
4. Submit feedback (thumbs up/down) to improve answer quality
