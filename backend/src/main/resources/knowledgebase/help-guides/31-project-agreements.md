# Project Agreements

A place to log "we agreed X" decisions that aren't work items — not everything discussed in a meeting belongs on the Task board.

## Where to Find It

Open **Meetings** from the sidebar. The **Project Agreements** section sits below the meetings list.

## What It's For

Some outcomes of a discussion are agreements or terms the team has settled on — a review cadence, an on-call ownership split, a scope boundary — rather than something to build. Logging these as Tasks pollutes the backlog with nothing to actually do; logging them nowhere means they get forgotten or re-litigated. Project Agreements is a simple, dated, append-over-time log for exactly this: each entry has a title, the agreement text, and the date it was reached.

## Creating an Agreement

1. Click **Add Agreement**.
2. Fill in a short **title** and the **agreement text**.
3. The **agreed date** defaults to today — change it if you're logging something reached earlier.
4. Optionally link it to the **meeting** it came out of. Most agreements won't have one — that's fine, plenty of decisions get made outside a formal meeting.
5. Save.

## Editing and Removing

Each entry has edit and delete actions. Deleting is a soft delete — the record is retained, not permanently erased.

## Requirements

Listing, creating, editing, and deleting agreements uses the same project-level permission as most other project management actions (ADMIN, MANAGER, MEMBER can read and write; READONLY can view).
