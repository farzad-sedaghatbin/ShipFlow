# Retrospectives

## What are Retrospectives?
Retrospectives are structured reflection sessions held after each cycle. They help teams identify what went well, what didn't, and what to improve.

## How to Create a Retrospective
1. Navigate to **Retrospectives** from the sidebar
2. Click **New Retrospective**
3. Select the cycle this retrospective is for
4. Choose the template format (e.g., Start/Stop/Continue, or custom)
5. Add items to each category

## Retrospective Templates
ShipFlow supports several retrospective formats:
- **Start/Stop/Continue** — What should we start doing, stop doing, and continue doing?
- **What Went Well / What Didn't** — Simple two-column format
- **Custom** — Create your own categories

## How to Conduct a Retrospective
1. Gather the team after the cycle ends (during cooldown)
2. Open the retrospective board
3. Each team member adds items anonymously
4. Discuss and vote on the most important items
5. Create action items from the top-voted topics
6. Action items carry forward to the next cycle

## Viewing Past Retrospectives
- All retrospectives are listed on the Retrospectives page
- Click any retrospective to see its full details and action items
- Track whether action items from previous retrospectives were addressed
