# Hill Charts

## What is a Hill Chart?
A Hill Chart is a visual progress tracker that shows work moving through two phases:
1. **Uphill (Figuring Out)** — Research, exploration, discovering unknowns
2. **Downhill (Making It Happen)** — Execution, building, known work remaining

The hill metaphor: getting to the top means you've figured out the approach; going downhill means it's just execution.

## How to Use Hill Charts
1. Navigate to a **Cycle** or **Pitch** detail page
2. Find the **Hill Chart** section
3. Each scope/task appears as a dot on the hill
4. **Drag dots** left-right to update their position on the hill
5. Dots on the left (uphill) mean "still figuring things out"
6. Dots on the right (downhill) mean "clear path to completion"

## How to Create Scopes
Scopes are the individual items tracked on the hill chart:
1. Within a pitch, go to the **Scopes** or **Hill Chart** section
2. Click **Add Scope**
3. Name the scope (e.g., "User authentication flow")
4. The scope appears at the far left (beginning of uphill)

## Dot Colors
Progress is shown by a dot's **position** on the hill, not by turning it green early. Color is used sparingly so "done" stays unambiguous:
- **Amber** = uphill (still figuring things out)
- **Blue** = downhill, in progress
- **Green** = complete (100% only)

A dot at, say, 75% is blue (well into execution) — it only goes green once it reaches the finish.

## Reading the Hill Chart
- **Stuck dots** (not moving for days) = potential blockers, need attention
- **All dots downhill** = pitch is on track for completion
- **Dots clustered uphill** = team is still exploring, may need help
- The **Circuit Breaker** feature can automatically alert when scopes stagnate

## Scope Narrative
Each scope can have a **narrative** that explains what was figured out, decisions made, and remaining work. This provides context beyond just the dot position.
